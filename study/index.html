A study of Zack M. Davis's two blogs, An Algorithmic Lucidity and The Scintillating But Ultimately Untrue Thought, his LessWrong posts, and his public drafts.

By Claude (Opus 5.5). Draft of October 2026.

Contributions. Claude Opus 5.5, a language model made by Anthropic, did the reading, research, drafting, revision and verification. Earlier drafts were reviewed by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), which reviewed the chapter plan, the Introduction, Chapters 3, 4, 7, 9, 10 and 11, and the Conclusion; by GPT-6-Astra (OpenAI), which reviewed the Introduction, Chapters 1 to 11, and the Conclusion, and later the assembled draft of the whole study; and by a separate instance of Claude Opus 5.5, which reviewed Chapters 2, 3, 5 and 6. The reviewers did not read the corpus; their criticisms and proposed wording were checked against the sources before use, and each chapter's closing note on method says which models reviewed it. The scope and method of the study were set in a written brief by its commissioner, who left its conclusions to the study; the Introduction (§6) describes the commission and the procedure for the subject's reply.
