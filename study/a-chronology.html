Apparatus AChronology

Events in Davis's life and writing that the study discusses, with the chapters that discuss them. Biographical facts are as the corpus states them. Titles are of posts unless marked as drafts; the bibliography gives their addresses.

1987
Born; grows up in California. Introduction
2005–2006
8 Mar 2005: a diary entry, at seventeen, records the erotic fantasy of having a female body, some three years after it began. Aug 2006: a notebook entry on finding the word autogynephilia on Wikipedia. Ch. 2
2007–2010
2007: finds Yudkowsky's writing on Overcoming Bias, at nineteen. End of 2007: a breakdown over the final paper for a course on feminist theory; leaves the University of California at Santa Cruz. Introduction; Ch. 9
2008: enrolls at a career college and quits within a term; is rebuked by Michael Vassar in the comments of Overcoming Bias, an episode he later cites as formative. Ch. 9; Ch. 6
2009: unpaid work for the Singularity Institute. Introduction; Ch. 11
2011
25 Dec: An Algorithmic Lucidity begins with "The Derivative of the Natural Logarithm." Ch. 1
2012
Returns to university at San Francisco State. Introduction
Jul–Aug: "Lies and Delusion"; "Contemporary," on the definition of virginity. Ch. 1; Ch. 4; Ch. 5
Nov–Dec: the renunciation of moral outrage and political argument ("Egoism as Defense Against a Life of Unending Heartbreak," "Speaking of Addiction," "Counterfactual Social Thought"); "Goodhart's World." Ch. 1; Ch. 11
2013
Feb: first psychiatric hospitalization, after a psychotic episode; leaves San Francisco State. Apr–Jun: "Prodrome," "Retirement." Ch. 1; Ch. 9
Autumn: attends a coding bootcamp; afterward works as a software engineer. Ch. 1
2014–2015
17 Nov 2014: the Star Trek: Voyager dialogue asking that coercion be described as coercion. Ch. 1; Ch. 9
21 Nov 2014: Scott Alexander's "The Categories Were Made For Man, Not Man For The Categories." Ch. 4
11 Oct 2015: "October Eleventh." Ch. 1; Ch. 8
2016
Mar: Yudkowsky's Facebook post estimating that at least 20% of the ones with penises are actually women (known as Davis quotes it). Ch. 7
Jul–Aug: "Concerns," "Identity," "Prescription." Ch. 1
Sep: The Scintillating But Ultimately Untrue Thought begins, under the pen name M. Taylor Saotome-Westlake. Introduction; Ch. 2; Ch. 3
1 Oct: "Reply to Ozymandias on Autogynephilia." 7 Oct: "Late-Onset." Ch. 2; Ch. 3; Ch. 1
Dec: begins an experiment with feminizing hormones; a post to the forum /r/GenderCritical, later kept as a hidden page. Ch. 2
2017
Feb: a second psychotic episode ends in a three-day involuntary detention. Ch. 9
Mar: "Dreaming of Political Bayescraft." Ch. 1; Ch. 10
Apr: resumes the hormones; a milder relapse; the search for a friend detained in another state. Jun: "Memoirs of My Recent Madness, Part I." Ch. 2; Ch. 9
Sep: stops the hormones ("Hormones Day 156," 27 Sep); "Grim Trigger." Dec: "Lesser-Known Demand Curves." Ch. 2; Ch. 8
2018
Jan: "Don't Negotiate With Terrorist Memeplexes." Introduction; Ch. 6
Feb: "Blegg Mode" and "The Categories Were Made for Man to Make Predictions." Jun: Ozy Brennan's "Man Should Allocate Some More Categories." Ch. 4
28 Nov: Yudkowsky's Twitter thread on pronouns, the memoir's turning point. From November, with a group of allies, Davis campaigns by email for a public clarification. Ch. 7
2019
13 Apr: "Where to Draw the Boundaries?" Ch. 4
Jun–Aug: "The Univariate Fallacy"; the question about "collaborative truthseeking"; "The Social Construction of Reality"; "Schelling Categories, and Simple Membership Tests." Ch. 3; Ch. 4; Ch. 6
Sep–Oct: "Heads I Win, Tails?—Never Heard of Her"; "Self-Identity Is a Schelling Point"; "Maybe Lying Doesn't Exist"; "Algorithms of Deception!" Ch. 5; Ch. 4
Dec: "Free Speech and Triskaidekaphobic Calculators"; "Firming Up Not-Lying Around Its Edge-Cases"; "Stupidity and Dishonesty Explain Each Other Away"; "Speaking Truth to Power Is a Schelling Point." Alexander appends a note to his 2014 essay. Ch. 10; Ch. 5; Ch. 4
2020
Jan: review of Cailin O'Connor's The Origins of Unfairness. 28 Apr: review of Charles Murray's Human Diversity. Ch. 10
Jun: Cade Metz of the New York Times contacts Davis; Alexander takes his blog down. Ch. 7
Jul–Aug: "Algorithmic Intent"; "Maybe Lying Can't Exist?!" Ch. 5
Sep: a final email to Yudkowsky, who publishes a clarification on Facebook; Davis declares himself no longer at war. Ch. 7
Dec: the "containment thread" disclosing the political context of "Where to Draw the Boundaries?"; a falling-out with Vassar's circle. Ch. 4; Ch. 7; Ch. 9
2021
Jan–Feb: tailcalled's "Causality Is Essential" and Davis's reply, "You Are Right and I Was Wrong"; "Unnatural Categories Are Optimized for Deception." Ch. 3; Ch. 4
Feb: the New York Times article on Alexander; Yudkowsky's proposal for pronoun reform. Ch. 10; Ch. 7
Mar: "Communication Requires Common Interests or Differential Signal Costs"; "Point Man." Ch. 5; Ch. 3
2 May: "Sexual Dimorphism in Yudkowsky's Sequences, in Relation to My Gender Problems," the first installment of the memoir. Ch. 2; Ch. 7
Jun: the dispute with Nate Soares over dolphins. Sep: "Blood Is Thicker Than Water." Ch. 4; Ch. 7
Oct: Alexander's comment on the "Vassarites" under Jessica Taylor's account of MIRI and CFAR, and Davis's reply. Ch. 9
2022
13 Mar: "Challenges to Yudkowsky's Pronoun Reform Proposal." Ch. 4; Ch. 7
Jul: "The Two-Type Taxonomy Is a Useful Approximation for a More Detailed Causal Model"; Alexander retracts his claim that Vassar made people psychotic. Ch. 3; Ch. 9
Nov: the Discord exchange with Yudkowsky transcribed in an unpublished draft. Ch. 7
2023
Feb: "'Rationalist Discourse' Is Like 'Physicist Motors.'" Ch. 6
Jul: drops the pseudonym (4 Jul); "Blanchard's Dangerous Idea and the Plight of the Lucid Crossdreamer" (8 Jul); "A Hill of Validity in Defense of Meaning" (15 Jul). Introduction; Ch. 2; Ch. 3; Ch. 7
Aug: "Assume Bad Faith." Ch. 6
Oct: the first dialogue between Doomimir and Simplicia; "Fake Deeply." Ch. 11
Dec: "Reply to Scott Alexander on Autogenderphilia"; "If Clarity Seems Like Death to Them." Ch. 3; Ch. 7
2024
2 Jan: states his "Empirical Claim," that late-onset gender dysphoria in males is not an intersex condition. Ch. 3
Feb–Jun: "And All the Shoggoths Merely Players"; "'Deep Learning' Is Function Approximation"; "Ironing Out the Squiggles"; "The Standard Analogy." Ch. 11
2 Mar: "Agreeing With Stalin in Ways That Exhibit Generally Rationalist Principles," the last published installment of the memoir. 26 Mar: publishes the transcript of his first interview with Metz. Ch. 7
15 Jul: transcript of a podcast with Phil Illy. Ch. 2; Ch. 3
2024–2025: returns to San Francisco State and finishes a degree in mathematics. Ch. 9
2025
22 Feb: drafts a message to the SEXNET list on sampling in a study by Hsu, Morandini and Rudd. Ch. 3
29 May: Duncan Sabien's "Truth or Dare," with a section on Davis. Ch. 6
22 Aug: Oliver Habryka bans Said Achmiz from LessWrong. Ch. 6
14 Sep: "A Generic Ontological Finickiness." 7 Oct: "Generalization and the Multiple Stage Fallacy?," the coda to the dialogues. Ch. 2; Ch. 4; Ch. 11
2026
Jan: "College Was Not That Terrible Now That I'm Not That Crazy" (1 Jan); in a review of the third dialogue, says which voice in it is his (21 Jan); "Disagreement Comes From the Dark World" (27 Jan). Ch. 9; Ch. 8; Ch. 6
13 Feb: "Hazards of Selection Effects on Approved Information." Ch. 6
Mar: "Prologue to Terrified Comments on Claude's Constitution" (8 Mar) and "Terrified Comments on Corrigibility in Claude's Constitution" (16 Mar). Ch. 11
30 May: "Comment on 'Banning Said Achmiz.'" Ch. 6
Jul: publishes transcripts of conversations with Metz (1–2 Jul); "Blogging Technology Interlude," on moving An Algorithmic Lucidity to a static site (21 Jul); the draft "Accidentally Misleading People Should Be Embarrassing" (28 Jul). Ch. 7; Introduction; Ch. 5
Sep: "Contra Piper on When Conversation Is Possible" (7 Sep); "Listening Disorders as Eating Disorders" (29 Sep). Ch. 5; Ch. 6
