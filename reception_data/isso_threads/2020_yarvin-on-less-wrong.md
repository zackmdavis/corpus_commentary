#### [-] tailcalled · 1 likes · 2021-01-10 · http://unremediatedgender.space/2020/Aug/yarvin-on-less-wrong/#isso-82
Really the problem with phrasing things as "Overcoming Bias" or "Less Wrong" is that it treats errors as being random perturbations to some basically correctly functioning system. And a lot of the time, for everyday life, they are; humans are finely tuned to handle everyday scenarios well. But once you leave the familiar area and go do more-abstract science (transsexual typologies? environmentalism vs hereditarianism? etc.) or more-unfamiliar tasks (FAI engineering?), you run into the fundamental problem: being right was never the default outcome that is merely prevented by noise and bias. It's a very specific narrow place, that you easily miss if you don't figure out good methods. It should more be thought of as desperately reaching out, trying to grab onto anything that could put you on the right path.

Definitely there are a lot of low-hanging truths on the right. But ok, you absorb them, then what? Now you've got twice as many important truths as when you only looked at the left. That's great, but how do we generate more truths?
