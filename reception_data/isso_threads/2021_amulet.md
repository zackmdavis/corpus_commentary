#### [>] Zack M. Davis · 2 likes · 2021-05-25 · http://unremediatedgender.space/2021/May/amulet/#isso-207
> admit it and attribute it to an innate male personality difference

Partially attribute it. Developmental psychology is complicated, and effects generally have more than one cause: the details of how men behave in a particular culture are going to depend on how that culture socializes its male children, which differs from culture to culture.

Unfortunately, however, I think that many of the things radical feminists don't like about men will turn out to be relatively more "innate"—that is, much easier to change by intervening on prenatal hormone levels rather than with better education and propaganda (as fond as I am of [classic 2nd-wave feminist propaganda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_to_Be..._You_and_Me))—and that this is knowable in advance through multiple lines of evidence: look at, for example, the universality of male dominance in the ethnographic literature (Stephen Goldberg has an [excellent](/2017/Jan/and-yet-none-more-blameable/) if depressing [book on this](https://www.amazon.com/Why-Men-Rule-Theory-Dominance/dp/0812692373)), and how [girls with congenital adrenal hyperplasia have more male-typical behavior](/images/cah_diffs_table.png), the [accounts of those couple dozen boys with cloacal extrophy who were raised as girls](/papers/reiner-psychosexual_development_in_genetic_males.pdf) ...

This doesn't mean that socialization doesn't matter at all—it matters, quantitatively. And it doesn't mean that any particular vision of a post-gender Utopia is impossible—just that, [getting there from here would require](/2017/Dec/theres-a-land-that-i-see-or-the-spirit-of-intervention/) either biological interventions (tinkering with prenatal hormones, or very advanced genetic engineering), or more intensive social interventions than most radfems are imagining. (As an extreme illustration of the latter, if you punished dominance behavior in boys with a savage whipping administered by a woman or a robot, then those boys would learn not to act that way.)

> the same gender-skewed math test scores

Sex differences in average math test scores are [already very small](https://psycnet.apa.org/record/2014-48297-001). (Cohen's d = 0.1 means a random pick from the higher-scoring group has a 52.8% chance of a higher score than a random pick from the lower-scoring group; you'd never notice without running the stats.) The gaps of cultural concern only show up at the high end. To the extent that this is due to the [greater male variance phenomenon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variability_hypothesis) (more male geniuses and idiots), it's not entirely obvious how this would be caused by stereotyping/socialization pressures (which we usually imagine dragging means around rather than variances)?
