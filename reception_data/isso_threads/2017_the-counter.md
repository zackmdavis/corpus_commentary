Well, point 1 shows that a difference could be transphobia, which fits quite well with the details of point 3. If you then apply a strong sparsity prior to this, then you get the "everything is fine except for the cotton ceiling" worldview.

Of course, that worldview is terrible! But I can't help but notice that critics, even you, engage in the same sorts of reasoning of pushing strong sparsity priors, just while trusting different alliances (leading to different sets of facts to talk about, and different goals to rationalize).

Ahem. So how do people manage to have a whole subculture about Truth and Empiricism and pretend not to notice these obvious facts about huge sweeping social changes in their own backyard?

Lack of access to ground truth seems like a basically insurmountable constraint; the only way out is through, by promoting better information sharing. Further, even if you have unrestricted access for querying facts, there's still "big picture" issues that a single individual, especially one who isn't involved in the spaces in question, will have a hard time dealing with. This can be dealt with through incremental diverse discussion, suggesting solutions and learning why they don't work.

The problem becomes, where to discuss it? LessWrong has anti-politics norms, for ultimately good reasons. Rationalists discuss politics on blogs, though, and the obvious blog to consider would be Ozy's, who seems to have addressed trans women's impact on lesbians to some degree? And then there's The Motte, which is basically the dedicated space for rat-adj people to discuss politics. As I understand, The Motte seems to be generally sympathetic to your concerns.

I think ultimately the big constraint/missing step is the following: blogs aren't really suited for combining multiple people's views, and subreddits aren't really suited for accumulating smaller discussions into bigger works. What is needed is not an alternative to rationalism, but some organization within rationalism that can properly promote truth about politically charged social topics, via

Transparent accumulation of truth and concerns,
Transparent synthesis of this truth and concerns into a bigger picture,
Transparent summaries for people who are not deeply involved in the debate to quickly get up to speed.
