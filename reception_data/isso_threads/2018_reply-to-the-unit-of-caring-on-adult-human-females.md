Basic arguments:

'woman' the only candidate for a word to pick out that category, which you'd expect would exist

We can sometimes know people were women by only knowing they're an adult human female

In a world of gender role reversals, 'adult human females' correctly picks out women but most other definitions do not

Explains stylistic use of 'woman' as near-equivalent to 'female' by many


Then goes on to critique counter-arguments.
