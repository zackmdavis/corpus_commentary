I feel like the Murray rule misses the point of this sort of standard. The goal of a standard is not to achieve consistency and gender equality, it exists for the same reason we have rules about capitalization: sentences are slightly jarring to read if they don't follow the convention we're used to. In a world following the Murray rule, half* the books say "he" while half* of them say "she" and the goals of standardization have been advanced barely more than if we just asked each author to flip a coin.

*Well not half, whatever the gender distribution of published works is.
