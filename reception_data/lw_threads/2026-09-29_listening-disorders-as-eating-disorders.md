#### [>] Viliam · 3 karma · 2026-09-30 · https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/gXWz2szs7jKSGLiRv/listening-disorders-as-eating-disorders?commentId=yRwKwftndA8ndTs6A
> Given that you're already advocating a certain opinion, I think the primary effect of advocating it rudely is probably to polarize people against you and make them take you less seriously.

I think rudeness communicates status. By treating your opponents rudely, you indirectly claim that their status is lower than yours.

This is why it is a mistake to engage with rude opponents. The audience may or may not be convinced of the truth of your words, but will take it as an admission that you indeed are lower status than your opponent.

(And for normies, status is way more important than truth.)

As an example, think about the atheism wars. At the beginning, many people were like "these atheists make a few good points, but why do they have to be so arrogant about the religion? if they were more polite, they would be less controversial, and maybe could make more people listen to them". But that impoliteness was strategic; it communicated that religion is not as high-status as it would like to be, and as a consequence, many people became encouraged to disagree with religion. That sounds like disagreeing with religion for the wrong reasons -- you should reject it for being false, not for not being as high-status as it used to be in the past -- but for many people, making the conclusion that something high status is false would be psychologically unbearable. (Also notice the asymmetry in the demand for respect. No one objected against e.g. priests treating atheists with contempt. As high-status people, it was their privilege to preach to the masses that their opponents are immoral and deserve to be tortured. It is only improper if a low-status person says something bad about a high-status person, not vice versa.)

So sometimes it is proper to say "my opponent is a retard", because you simultaneously express disagreement with their beliefs and with their status claims. Of course it is better if you also provide an explanation why your opponent is a retard.
