# External sources fetched and read for the Opus pilot chapter

All fetched 2026-10-16 into `pilot/opus/sources/<slug>/` (raw `page.html` or `paper.pdf`, text conversion `text.txt` via `tools/html_to_text.py` or `pdftotext`). Quotations in the chapter are checked against these text files. Reception material already gathered in Phase 1 (`reception_data/`, listed in `notes/sources.md`) was re-read firsthand for this chapter where cited.
