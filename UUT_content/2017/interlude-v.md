"I've been getting this mild headachey sensation a lot the past few days, especially when, for example, standing up suddenly. What could—" _(gasping excitedly)_ "Could it be the hormones actually doing something? Is this a _girl_ headache?"

"But a horrible cardiovascular _girl_ death!"
