Title: Exactly What It Says on the Tin
Date: 2016-10-29 20:33
Category: commentary
Tags: autogynephilia, cathartic

For many years I've been a fan of a certain genre of, um, adult blogs featuring captioned photographs of women, where the captions tell fantasy stories about how the women used to be men before some magical or technological intervention. The titles of the blogs often contain the word _transgender_ (or the abbreviation _TG_). I remember dimly thinking—never in so many words—_Gee, I imagine actual transgender people would be pretty offended if they knew that the word used to refer to_ them _was being used to describe_ this _kind of material!_

... _why_

What kind of pornography did I _think_ pre-trans women used?

_Why didn't anyone just_ tell _me?!_
