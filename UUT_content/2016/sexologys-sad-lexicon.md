Title: Sexology's Sad Lexicon
Date: 2016-11-06 18:52
Category: other
Tags: cathartic, poetry

(In honor of Chicago's victory in the recently-concluded 2016 World Series, with [apologies to Franklin Pierce Adams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baseball%27s_Sad_Lexicon#Composition).)

These are the saddest of possible names:  
&emsp;"Blanchard and Bailey and Lawrence."  
Trio of scholars on guys being dames,  
&emsp;Blanchard and Bailey and Lawrence.  
Ruthlessly pricking our gender identity  
&emsp;Making a trans woman face her reality—  
Names that survive despite all the world's enmity:  
&emsp;"Blanchard and Bailey and Lawrence."
