### The Social Construction of Reality and the Sheer Goddamned Pointlessness of Reason
- Path: `UUT_content/2019/the-social-construction-of-reality-and-the-sheer-goddamned-pointlessness-of-reason.md` · 2019-08-11 · commentary (bullet-biting, cathartic, epistemic horror) · 1704 w · http://unremediatedgender.space/2019/Aug/the-social-construction-of-reality-and-the-sheer-goddamned-pointlessness-of-reason/
- Summary: Epigraph: Suskind's "reality-based community." "Truth isn't real; there are only competing narratives" (hedged); widely believed lies change the social part of reality ("bootstrap themselves into being true"). Dominant coalitions keep frame control via motte-and-bailey "language-mindfuckery games, justified by utilitarianism" ("trans women are women, where by 'women' I mean people who identify as women"); utilitarian calculations determined by "whichever utility monster has made itself most politically salient." Claims "Autogynephilic males are better at large-scale coalitional politics than actual lesbians for basically the same reasons that men-in-general are better at coalitional politics than women-in-general," so AGPs won the conflict over the word. Sex/gender distinction a ~40-year second-wave artifact; post-*Obergefell* "Blue Egregore" LGBT machinery absorbed ~5% (?) of men with intense AGP and ~5% (?) of girls losing female intrasexual competition (ROGD link). Simulacra levels (Hoffman/Taylor after Baudrillard): 20th-c. transsexualism levels 1–2; post-*Obergefell* gender theory level 3. Personality differences Mahalanobis D≈2.7 (del Giudice). "A dream about the use of maps" — but "humans _live in the map_."
- Themes: social-construction, narratives, motte-and-bailey, simulacra, coalitional-politics, autogynephilia, rogd, blue-egregore, sex-gender-distinction, epistemic-horror
- Xrefs: UUT 2023 "Blanchard's Dangerous Idea…" (rapid-onset; AGP prevalence claims to check against literature). Empirical claims flagged for research phase: "~5% of men with intense AGP", AGP-vs-lesbian coalitional advantage, Mahalanobis D≈2.7 (Del Giudice et al. 2012).
- Quotes:
> And so sufficiently-widely-believed lies _bootstrap themselves into being true._
> In my secularized world which is at least nominally managed under the auspices of Reason, the preferred tactic is clever [motte-and-bailey](http://web.archive.org/web/20200529221511/https://slatestarcodex.com/2014/11/03/all-in-all-another-brick-in-the-motte/) language-mindfuckery games, justified by utilitarianism
> Thus, trans women are women, where by 'women' I mean people who identify as women.
> [Autogynephilic males](http://www.annelawrence.com/autogynephilia_&_MtF_typology.html) are better at large-scale coalitional politics than actual lesbians for basically the same reasons that men-in-general are better at coalitional politics than women-in-general
> In contrast, post-_Obergefell_ gender theory belongs to simulacrum level 3: rather than having a non-circular truth condition, "gender" is just a free-floating Schelling point
> But humans don't use maps to navigate the territory. Humans _live in the map_.

### Self-Identity Is a Schelling Point
- Path: `UUT_content/2019/self-identity-is-a-schelling-point.md` · 2019-10-06 · commentary (categorization, game theory) · 1456 w · http://unremediatedgender.space/2019/Oct/self-identity-is-a-schelling-point/
- Summary: Steelman of self-identity criterion using "Schelling Categories": when gender must have a canonical answer and passing is blurry, deferring to the person is the natural Schelling point (as with very androgynous people). Slippery slope between domains: "Sex is immutable ... Y chromosome" and "Self-reported self-identity only" are both stable Schelling points; compromise systems are unstable. "Policy debates should not appear one-sided"; "What did you take me for, some kind of _partisan hack?!_"
- Themes: schelling-points, self-identification, steelmanning, policy-stability, categorization
- Xrefs: AAL 2019 "Schelling Categories" (his own, cited as "my favorite author on _Less Wrong_"); UUT draft `assigned-at-birth-is-a-schelling-point.md`.
- Quotes:
> Interestingly, a extension of this line of reasoning suggests an apparently novel argument in _favor_ of the self-identity criterion
> If, despite this, [social reality](/2019/Aug/the-social-construction-of-reality-and-the-sheer-goddamned-pointlessness-of-reason/) continues to require the question to have a definite canonical answer, and we can't appeal to "passing" because that's too subjective and blurry, the natural Schelling point is, "Just _ask_ the person what gender they are, and that's what they are."
> In contrast, any of a number of "compromise" systems, while potentially performing better on edge cases, suffer from ambiguity and are on that account less game-theoretically stable.

### Reply to Ozymandias on Fully Consensual Gender
- Path: `UUT_content/2019/reply-to-ozymandias-on-fully-consensual-gender.md` · 2019-12-20 · commentary (categorization, Ozy) · 2144 w · http://unremediatedgender.space/2019/Dec/reply-to-ozymandias-on-fully-consensual-gender/
- Summary: Reply (18 months late) to Ozy's "Man Should Allocate Some More Categories" (2018-06-18), focusing on its money analogy. Likes the analogy but notes money's forms are constrained; "fully consensual" money (banana as $20,000 bill; defaced Canadian $5 as US) → Gresham's law: people will lie and route around. Likewise "fully consensual gender": people will make decisions treating non-passing trans women as men while not admitting it. "Consent" fits overwhelming asymmetries (sex), not categorization, where resolving for the modeled means "mind control, or at least compelled speech." Dating-site example: "How dumb do you think I am?!" Coherent ≠ feasible. Utilitarian grounds are constrained by facts. Kipling epigraph (paraphrased). Footnote: Blanchard proposed "subjective sex"; online, fully consensual gender is easier.
- Themes: ozy, consensual-gender, money-analogy, greshams-law, compelled-speech, feasibility, categorization, dating
- Xrefs: Ozy, "Man Should Allocate Some More Categories" (thingofthings, 2018-06-18) — fetch; draft `UUT_content/drafts/i-mean-yes-i-agree-that-man-should-allocate-some-more-categories-but.md`.
- Quotes:
> _They will be lying_. In marketplaces governed by "trans American dollars _are_ American dollars" social norms, smart buyers will prefer to buy with defaced Canadian dollars
> If there's a conflict between one person's desire to be modeled as belonging to a particular gender and someone else's perception that the person is more accurately thought of as belonging to a different gender, then it's not clear what it would _mean_ to resolve the conflict in the direction of "consent of the modeled" other than mind control, or at least compelled speech.
> I happily agree that fully consensual gender is a _coherent_ position. That doesn't make it _feasible_.
> Until then, the question remains: how dumb do you think we are?!
