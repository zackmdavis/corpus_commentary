### Prediction Markets Are Not a Drop-In Replacement for Concepts

Keltham's behavior is "nearest-unblocked strategy": "minimize sexism while being sensitive to outcome-based pragmatic constraints." Yudkowsky's Discord "Word of God" ("Keltham isn't proposing to actually enforce...") disputed. "This is a _heresy_ blog, not an _apostate_ blog." The Star Trek "Omega Glory" Constitution analogy: contingent values vs. convergent strategies. Anti-discrimination is a game-theoretic Schelling point that American progressivism reified as a terminal value, like NGDP targeting, an un–dath ilani hack.
