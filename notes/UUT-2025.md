### Interview with Kelsey Piper on Self-Censorship and the Vibe Shift
- Path: `UUT_content/2025/interview-with-kelsey-piper-on-self-censorship-and-the-vibe-shift.md` · 2025-08-06 · other (Kelsey Piper, censorship, discourse, politics) · 4679 w · http://unremediatedgender.space/2025/Aug/interview-with-kelsey-piper-on-self-censorship-and-the-vibe-shift/
- Summary: Edited transcript of a 17 July 2025 recorded conversation (audio at `/media/2025-07-17-kelsey_piper_interview.ogg`). Most of the words are Piper's; quote her as Piper, not Davis. Prompted by Piper's post-election "lesbian fire chief" tweet: Piper says she wouldn't have tweeted it had Harris won, and diagnoses "cowardice, being unwilling to get mild social disapproval" over the previous four years about small true things, though "I always said true things I thought were really important." Neoliberals vs. progressives: "the neoliberals are right on nearly all of those disagreements"; the parties' factions should be legible. On the right: Christian conservatives vs. the "woke right"; Clinton's 2016 pied-piper strategy was "corrosive and kind of evil." Davis asks about conflicts between clarity and team victory. Piper: loud honesty in this environment "feels like fake posturing of honesty"; the real problem is choosing which lies to respond to (engagement-farming tweets on mixed-race relatedness); "everybody is doing" selective reporting. Davis cites Scott Alexander to Yarvin on the "vibe shift" being an "autochthonous liberal victory"; Piper "more than half disagree[s]"; discusses affirmative action (supports the opportunity-adjusted version but thinks it is infeasible; agrees with the Supreme Court). Davis suggests neoliberals might claim credit after the fact; Piper says woke was its own phenomenon (Tumblr, academia, nonprofits) and both factions trying to pin it on the other is itself evidence of retreat. The transcript ends mid-topic ("And there's other stuff that didn't."), possibly an excerpt.

- Xrefs: UUT 2018 "Reply to The Unit of Caring"; UUT 2023 "A Hill of Validity" and "If Clarity" (Piper episodes). **Reception relevance:** Piper's own voice; note she does not address the earlier disputes here.

### A Generic Ontological Finickiness
- Path: `UUT_content/2025/a-generic-ontological-finickiness.md` · 2025-09-14 · commentary (Eliezer Yudkowsky, categorization, sex differences) · 2951 w · http://unremediatedgender.space/2025/Sep/a-generic-ontological-finickiness/
- Summary: Yudkowsky's Twitter thread offered four theories of why MtF draws more opprobrium than FtM, and dismissed a "theory #0" of "generic ontological finickiness" ("*mike drop*"). Davis anticipated theories #3 and #4 in a 2018 footnote. His historical counterexamples to Yudkowsky's claim that FtM once drew more outrage: Deborah Sampson, Charley Parkhurst, Murray Hall, with period obituaries. Davis: no one claims #0 is the whole story; different people have different motives (gender-critical feminists, conservatives like Michael Knowles, "philosophers like the present writer or Alex Byrne"); a one-dimensional projection creates false contradictions (Yudkowsky and Gebru are both "anti-AI": "Mic drop!"). Policy and epistemics intertwine; the pyrite/gold sequence ("People who claim that pyrite is gold because they define _gold_ as whatever sellers identify as gold, are lying or confused"); a "trans gold" shrug must be genuine, not coerced. RustConf: a table of nine with five or six trans women: "Am I supposed to pretend not to notice?" Answers theory #3: "if it were cheap and easy to swap into a real female body, I would absolutely transition." Puts "a decent chunk of probability" on women on average having less of the cognitive repertoires behind wanting to write Rust; footnote asks whether that feeling is "implied misogyny." "I think a lot of us like being the kind of status-seeking gynephilic systematizers that we often are" ("us" here groups Davis with trans women). Ends: "rationalists _should_" care about descriptive accuracy.
- Themes: yudkowsky-critique, categorization, pyrite-gold, motives-vs-truth, sex-differences-interests, rustconf, history-of-ftm, philosophy-of-language
- Xrefs: UUT 2018 "Categories ... Predictions" fn 7; UUT 2024 "Agreeing With Stalin" #it-matters...; UUT 2023 "Beyond the Binary"; UUT 2022 "Challenges" (Kerr).
- Quotes:
> As someone who [_has_ expressed a deep generic horror of things being described as other than what they are and criticized gender identity ideology on those specific grounds]
> When philosophers like the present writer or [Alex Byrne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Byrne_(philosopher)#Writing) criticize gender identity theories as describing things as other than what they are, they're expressing a devotion to the truth, as philosophers do.
> Nevertheless, pyrite is not gold.
> People who claim that pyrite is gold because they define _gold_ as whatever sellers identify as gold, are lying or confused.
> Fine. But it has to _actually_ be good enough. The shrug has to be genuine, not coerced
> _Why?_ The joint gender and assigned-sex-at-birth composition of that table can't possibly be a coincidence. Am I supposed to pretend not to notice?
> But theory #3's focus on visibility and perceptual cues is missing the commenter's point. If the medtech got much better, [if it were cheap and easy to swap into a real female body, I would absolutely transition]
> It still feels ugly to say even now—it's not something I _want_ to be true—but if I'm being honest, I have to put a decent chunk of probability on the hypothesis that women on average have less of whatever [cognitive repertoires]
> I think a lot of us like being the kind of status-seeking gynephilic systematizers that we often are.
> Maybe human beings don't always, inherently, consistently care about exact descriptive accuracy—but rationalists _should_.
