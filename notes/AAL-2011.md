### The Derivative of the Natural Logarithm
- Path: `AAL_content/2011/the-derivative-of-the-natural-logarithm.md` · 2011-12-25 · mathematics (calculus) · 1224 w · https://zackmdavis.net/blog/2011/Dec/the-derivative-of-the-natural-logarithm/
- Summary: Mock-gothic exposition ("dear reader", "true secret") of why 1/x is defined for negative x though ln x is not: the complex logarithm of a negative number is ln x₀ + (2n+1)πi, and the constant imaginary part drops out on differentiation. Contains an aside that "function" is a convention: calling the inverse of a non-injective map a "multifunction" is about clear communication, not about what is true. Ends by asking the reader to "cut me some goddam slack" for a non-rigorous argument.
- Themes: mathematics, definitions-and-categories (early), comic-voice
- Xrefs: earliest instance of the "definitions are conventions; facts are not" motif later central to categorization writing (cf. AAL 2019 "Where to Draw the Boundaries?").
- Quotes:
> it is ultimately only a _definition_, a convention for what we mean when we use the world _function_, and mere definitions can't coerce true facts into being something other than what they are.
> (One can even imagine that if the history of mathematical inquiry had gone differently, we might call multifunctions _functions_ and functions (say) _deterministic functions_, although some would argue that it is useless and idle to speculate about worlds that are not our own.)

### Dialogue Concerning Birthdays
- Path: `AAL_content/2011/dialogue-concerning-birthdays.md` · 2011-12-28 · fiction · 530 w · https://zackmdavis.net/blog/2011/Dec/dialogue-concerning-birthdays/
- Summary: First Synthia/Quiana dialogue. Synthia (hyper-literal, rationalist, says "Newtonmas") questions why celebrations attach to calendar dates rather than accomplishments; a "school graduation party" example produces a flicker of pain on her face. Quiana points out "Happy birthday" is just a pleasantry; Synthia then simply says "Thanks." Comedy of the over-literal mind learning the function of social speech.
- Themes: fiction-dialogue, social-convention, schooling (glancing), literalism
- Xrefs: Synthia/Quiana recur in 2012 ("The Morality of Ringing a Bell"). "Schelling point" vocabulary used already.
- Quotes:
> I can't think of a better Schelling point. Rather, my question is this: if a world without celebrations is too terrible to imagine, why do we have celebrations for no reason other than the calendar date?
