# Changelog: first edition (v1) to second edition (v2)

Every change to `study/` after the v1 freeze (`study-v1/`, 2026-10-22), with
its cause: **erratum** (an error of fact, quotation or citation, confirmed
against the source), **conceded point** (a change made because an objection
in the author's reply was judged to hold, in whole or in part), or
**unrelated improvement**. Rebutted objections change no text but are
recorded in a note in the chapter where they are material; those notes are
listed here too. The reasoning for each decision is in the response file
named for the installment.
