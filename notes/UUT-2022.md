### Challenges to Yudkowsky's Pronoun Reform Proposal
- Path: `UUT_content/2022/challenges-to-yudkowskys-pronoun-reform-proposal.md` · 2022-03-13 · commentary (Eliezer Yudkowsky, convention) · 12012 w · http://unremediatedgender.space/2022/Mar/challenges-to-yudkowskys-pronoun-reform-proposal/
- Summary: Epigraph Raleigh's "The Lie." Bolded summary up front. Target: Yudkowsky's Feb 2021 Facebook post (facebook.com/yudkowsky/posts/10159421750419228 — login-walled; quoted only via Davis) proposing "'He' refers to the set of people who have asked us to use 'he', with a default for those-who-haven't-asked that goes by gamete size" as "the normative definition," because baking controversial definitions into the protocol is "_logically_ rude." Davis agrees English's gendered pronouns are bad design, but: (1) the default still bakes in sex; (2) trans people want pronouns *because* pronouns convey sex category — "functionally 'hypocritical'" (worthless-toy analogy); T–V distinction (tú/usted) and Ms. show reformers abolish distinctions rather than circularly redefine them; Hofstadter's 1983 "A Person Paper on Purity in Language" never proposed it — "why not?"; wireheading analogy; trans activists on Twitter themselves want belief, not mere compliance. (3) "that you insist everybody use" is DARVO given who actually insists; one-sided "freedom of pronouns" framing. (4) Meaning = usage ("dog," "billion" = 2001, null-terminated strings; backwards-compatibility trap; 370M native speakers). Barra Kerr's "Pronouns are Rohypnol" (Stroop effect) — Kerr doesn't claim pronouns are "lies"; Yudkowsky attacks the weak form. CLIP typographic attacks: "[Pronoun badges] are, quite literally, a typographic attack against English speakers' brains" (value-free). Critics call Davis "she" — he never corrects them because it conveys their meaning. **The "Oliver" self-report**: Yudkowsky: "I do not know what it feels like from the inside to feel like a pronoun is attached to something in your head much more firmly than 'doesn't look like an Oliver'"; Davis: can't take it literally (sex recognition 96%; no "secondary Oliver characteristics"; *Amazing World of Gumball* pregnant-turtle joke; CFAI 2001 footnote 16 "I flip a coin to determine whether a given human is male or female"; 2009 "Hero With a Thousand Chances" comment "I couldn't make Aerhien a man"). "The sheer _chutzpah_": the project is precisely to let trans people's feelings control the protocol. De-gendering vs. trans-rights are different projects. Honest alternative: "sucks to be you." Concedes self-ID is a Schelling point (cf. 2019 post), as is sex-only; moderates are in trouble. "I don't misgender people! (In public. Only rarely in private ...)" **Vignette**: rape victim ordered by judge to say "_Her_ erect penis"; judge sneers "You are _ontologically_ confused ... LambdaMOO." Defends realism (cites fairplayforwomen data; Canadian father jailed). "This is _not_ the woke position" quote → "an _incoherent_ position that's optimized to concede to the woke the behavior that they want for a _different stated reason_." Honest options include "use whatever pronoun convention happens to be dominant in your local social environment … You don't have to invent _absurd lies_". Why 12k words of vitriol: he needs correct reasoning for medical/social decisions; HRT was a good decision; asking for she/her would be "_obviously incredibly bad_"; naïve versions of himself harmed by bad information — "I'm better off because I don't trust Eliezer Yudkowsky to tell the truth." "(I guess I can't say I wasn't warned.)"

Warns against unfalsifiable exceptions. Evidence for six arrows (Lippa; Bailey & Zucker; Lawrence ETLE). "very bullish on thinking in graphical models rather than discrete taxons." Thanks Tailcalled.
- Themes: two-type-taxonomy, causal-graphs, liability-threshold, etle, cultural-factors, falsifiability, theory-refinement
- Xrefs: UUT 2021 Tailcalled reply (fulfills that debt partially); Backlog Metablogging (owed etiology post).
