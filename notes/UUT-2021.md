> But as Tailcalled's response points out, this is just wrong! Different causal theories can generate the same correlations in a particular set of observations, while still making meaningfully and drastically different claims about the world.

- Summary: Zhao Gao's "point deer make horse" loyalty test. Interlocutor (paraphrased) says pronoun choice "conveys very relevant information about the speaker and their attitudes to trans people" — so new norms optimize information about the speaker: a loyalty test, costlier the more absurd. - Themes: loyalty-tests, pronouns, signaling, coalition-politics, rogd, autogynephilia, transhumanism
- Quotes:
> I see. So the [new norms](/2018/Oct/sticker-prices/) are optimized to convey information _about the speaker_ [rather than what is being spoken about](http://thetranswidow.com/2021/02/21/pronouns-and-the-purpose-of-language/).
> And the less intuitive it is, the better it works _as_ a loyalty test
> I'm not suggesting a conspiracy, of course; just the design signature of [cultural evolution](/2020/Jan/book-review-the-origins-of-unfairness/).

> I'm glad that sex reassignment exists for those who need it, or just _want_ it. (I would want it if the technology were better.)

- Summary: Major autobiographical-intellectual memoir, first of the sequence later continued in the 2023–24 memoir posts ("A Hill of Validity…" etc.). Epigraph *Hamilton* "Hurricane" ("I'll write my way out"). Calls rationalism his "robot cult" (phrase from Dale Carrico) out of bitterness at "almost everyone I used to trust" (long story deferred). **Biography:** found *Overcoming Bias* summer 2007 at 19 (probably via Megan McArdle); "Yudkowsky rewrote my personality over the internet." Since age 13–14, sole masturbation fantasy = magical transformation into a woman; *Ranma ½* origin of pen-surname "Saotome"; first told his Diary ~3 years later; non-sexual "beautiful pure sacred self-identity thing" (notebook persona, feminine handwriting, pink sheets, *Whipping Girl* in 2007, attempted initials-name at UCSC, ponytail, "tranny girl" song fragment). Found "autogynephilia" on Wikipedia 2006. Teenage "antisexism" = "psychological-sex-differences denialism," a quasi-religion (*Moral Animal* horror; song wishing death on advice columnist Amy Alkon — now "_abhorrent_"); 2008 OB comment ("exactly the sort of psychology that leads to rape?") drew a ban suggestion from the soon-to-be SIAI President, same day as first crossdressing in front of friends (purple dress). Motive for feminism: "It's because I was _straight_" — identificatory love of women. **Sequences on sex:** "Fake Utility Functions" (love of a man for a woman not derived from…); psychological unity of humankind + Trivers ("two designs"); sexual dimorphism evolves >1.5 OOM slower (Rogers–Mukherjee); "Interpersonal Entanglement," "Failed Utopia #4-2" (verthandi deserve a different word than "women" — precision); his own comment of horror then; "Changing Emotions" (encountered its 2004 Extropians original in June 2008: "Am I shocked, liberated, relieved, scared, angry, amused?"); "normal, masculine man wearing a female body like a suit of clothing" is, for his fantasy, "the _ideal_ outcome." **Statistics of sex:** transformation booth thought experiment (Nana Visitor c. 1998; "morphic adaptation unit"); brain-volume d ≈ 3.0/2.1; percentile-mapping (5′11″ → 5′5½″); typical set / multivariate shell; XX/XY as "treasure map" definition, SRY as root of causal graph; face sexing (96%, d≈3.5; 99.47% model); GAN latent-space hyperplanes, FaceApp "the _uniquely best piece of software in the world_"; Joel et al. mosaic vs. del Giudice critique; MRI 96.8%; skeleton analogy. Self-assessment: "a point in the _male_ region"; "most of the _women_ in my open-source programming scene are male"; Su et al. things–people d≈0.93; "I think everything is a system ... because I'm male??"; WISC-III image ("objectively not that smart"); X factor as male-only mental-illness pattern; sister (chem Ph.D., SAT comparison) as vector for female analogue. **Preference:** if one-shot, doesn't want to be "Actually Female" — wants "a normal [...] man wearing a female body like a suit of clothing"; "Is that weird? Is that wrong?" Phenomenology: imagines a specific woman from outside, wanting "the soul behind those eyes to be _me_"; mirror; James Cantor's mirror-neuron speculation; trio of clues (not repulsed by own body; indiscriminate about female bodies; other male bodies repulsive) fit erotic target location error; ETLE analogues (apotemnophilia, autopedophilia, furries, agalmatophilia); early-teen dog/horse/statue fantasies; "a bug in _male_ sexuality" (bug w.r.t. evolution, not morality); autogynephilia "_intrinsically self-undermining_." Different-books-different-order counterfactual. HRT: small breasts a quality-of-life improvement; would pay $300k for real transformation; programmer's fear of dependencies; dilation; ponytail/HRT/laser as "smart moves"; FFS/latex masks musing; staying on HRT and social transition not smart; Berkeley/Portland "_they will be lying_. I know, because I lie." **Personal identity:** his 2008 comment "Is it cheating if you deliberately define your personal identity such that the answer is _No_?" — now: yes, cheating; Lipschitz condition in ordinary personal identity; Sorites "incredibly boring"; Phineas Gage. **Values:** earlier resistance to Yudkowsky's metaethics because of antisexism; three causal parents (autogynephilia, sensitive boy, antisexist ideology); "_none of this shit is true_"; commit to the features of psychology that make the ideology seem good, not the ideology; 2017 psychosis note "cooperate with men who cooperate with women…". Close: ironic — wouldn't blog this unless his obsession were at the center of a defining political issue.

> That's why defining your personal identity to get the answer you want is cheating. If the answer you wanted was actually _true_, you could just say so without needing to _want_ it.
> When I introspect on the _causes_ of my whole gender ... _thing_, I see three parents in the causal graph: autogynephilia, being a sensitive boy rather than a macho guy, and my ideological commitment to antisexism
> The thing to be committed to is not any potentially flawed object-level ideology, like antisexism or Christianity, but [the features of human psychology that](http://zackmdavis.net/blog/2017/03/dreaming-of-political-bayescraft/) make the object-level ideology _seem like a good idea_.
