### Ozy, "On Autogynephilia" (Thing of Things, 13 Jul 2016) and comments (read in full)
- Ozy: Blanchard–Bailey theory "clearly untrue"; claims denial is a common
  trait, "making their theory (based primarily on self-report) utterly
  unfalsifiable– the definition of bad science." Three-way split: ordinary
  female sexuality / manifestation of dysphoria / "true autogynephiles".
- Davis comment (13 Jul 2016): "utterly unfalsifiable is too strong"; a
  correlation between socially desirable responding and self-reports about X
  would discriminate. Ozy: "Eh, fair", but that correlation can't separate
  "some hide" from "all are".
- Commenters (Maxim Kovalev, Henry Gorman, throwaway, veronica d) give
  first-person data points both ways. tailcalled 2017: in his surveys
  intensity of AGP tracks desire to be female.

### External (see sources.md, Ch. 2)
- Serano 2020 (full): objections (3) temporal order from self-report, (5)
  unfalsifiability via dismissal as misreporting; embodiment-fantasies model;
  "crossdresser stage"; cohort change; the two-stereotype argument; theory
  embraced "for ideological or sociological reasons".
- Serano & Veale 2022 (full): measurement invariance ("apples and
  sparkplugs"); the definitional dilemma (broad vs narrow AGP).
- Bettcher 2014 (abstract in English; body in Chinese translation with
  English terms): interest/attraction distinction; interactional account;
  "attraction to oneself" unintelligible, so "autogynephilia" a misleading
  term; self-exclusive fantasies are non-pathological "erotic fragments";
  explicitly not adjudicating Blanchard's causal/typological claims (defers to
  Moser and Serano); erasing trans people's own accounts of motivation one
  reason sexualized-motive reduction is implausible; orientation includes a
  gendered erotic self (gyne-/andro-reflexive).
- IEP "Epistemic Injustice" (Sinclair): testimonial injustice =
  identity-prejudicial credibility deficit; hermeneutical injustice = "some
  significant area of one's social experience obscured from collective
  understanding owing to a structural identity prejudice in the collective
  hermeneutical resource" (Fricker 2007, 155); paradigm: sexual harassment
  before the concept; credibility excess literature.
- Nisbett & Wilson 1977 (garbled scan; abstract & conclusions): little or no
  introspective access to higher-order processes; reports rest on a priori
  causal theories; accurate when the true cause is salient and plausible.
- Illy transcript line 428: Illy calls it "an epistemic injustice that people
  with this orientation don't get a fair shot at learning about it".
