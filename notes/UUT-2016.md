### Psychology Is About Invalidating People's Identities
- Path: `UUT_content/2016/psychology-is-about-invalidating-peoples-identities.md` · 2016-09-05 · commentary (bullet-biting, epistemology) · 364 w · http://unremediatedgender.space/2016/Sep/psychology-is-about-invalidating-peoples-identities/
- Summary: Self-reports are evidence, not the only evidence; social rule against contradicting self-descriptions shouldn't bind science, which must allow people to have false beliefs about themselves. Having identity "invalidated by _new information_ is an opportunity for growth." Angels analogy: respect the experience, deny the interpretation.
- Themes: self-report-fallibility, science-vs-politeness, identity, epistemology, autogynephilia (implicit)
- Xrefs: AAL 2016 "Identity"; UUT "Very Reassuring" links here.
- Quotes:
> we _cannot_ commit ourselves to the assumption that all self-reports must be taken as literally true, because—even if no one is deliberately _lying_, even if everyone is trying their very hardest to choose the words that will best express the ineffable truth of their subjective experience—that would exclude the vast swathes of hypothesis-space under which some people have false beliefs about themselves.
> And we can respect this person and trust that they're telling the truth about their subjective experiences, while at the same time stating confidently: those weren't actually angels.

### Reply to Ozymandias on Autogynephilia
- Path: `UUT_content/2016/reply-to-ozy-on-agp.md` · 2016-10-01 · commentary (autogynephilia, Ozy) · 823 w · http://unremediatedgender.space/2016/Oct/reply-to-ozy-on-agp/
- Summary: Point-by-point reply to Ozy's "On Autogynephilia" (Thing of Things, 2016-07-13). Blanchard's skepticism about autoandrophilia is a separate question from the MtF taxonomy (not a theory of trans men). "May or may not be correlated" is inadequate: taxonomy summarizes correlations; Cox/Mock/Rivera vs. Serano/McCloskey/Jenner clusters. Unfalsifiability charge rejected: rejecting all hypotheses predicting self-denial guarantees never learning the truth if people do deny. Asks what distinguishes "dysphoric" from "true" autogynephilia. Coda: political incentives to dismiss autogynephilia mislead the next generation — the mildly dysphoric boy who spent "_ten years_" assuming his problems couldn't be in the same taxon "might feel a _little bit betrayed_"; "the transgender community at large has been systematically lying to him, or ... is so systematically delusional that they might as well have been lying"; hence "an entire pseudonymous blog."
- Themes: autogynephilia, two-type-taxonomy, ozy, falsifiability, self-report-fallibility, betrayal-by-community, motivation-for-blog, correlation-not-one
- Xrefs: External: Ozy, "On Autogynephilia" (thingofthings.wordpress.com, 2016-07-13) — to fetch for reception/context. UUT drafts `falsifiability-and-what-we-expect-not-to-see.md`.
- Quotes:
> "May or may not be correlated"?! That's all you have to say?! Summarizing correlations is the _entire point_ of making a taxonomy.
> If you categorically reject all hypotheses that predict that sometimes people deny true propositions about themselves, then you will _never learn the truth_ if you happen to live in a world where people sometimes deny true propositions about themselves!
> But here's the thing: you _can't_ mislead the general public without thereby also misleading the next generation of trans-spectrum people.
> he might feel a _little bit betrayed_ when it turns out that it's _not_ clearly untrue and that the transgender community at large has been systematically lying to him, or, worse, is so systematically delusional that they might as well have been lying. In fact, he might be so upset as to be motivated to start an entire pseudonymous blog dedicated to dismantling your shitty epistemology!
