### An Intuition on the Bayes-Structural Justification for Free Speech Norms
- Path: `AAL_content/2017/an-intuition-on-the-bayes-structural-justification-for-free-speech-norms.md` · 2017-03-08 · philosophy · 260 w · https://zackmdavis.net/blog/2017/Mar/an-intuition-on-the-bayes-structural-justification-for-free-speech-norms/
- Summary: Speech = positive-sum information component + zero-sum social-control component; coalitions that let members share information outcompete; punishing the control component is tempting but risky because members will "gerrymander the _hate speech_ category boundary in the service of their own values."
- Themes: free-speech, bayes-structure, category-gerrymandering, social-control
- Xrefs: Category gerrymandering → "Where to Draw the Boundaries?" (2019), "Unnatural Categories Are Optimized for Deception" (2021). LW linkpost 2017-03-09.
- Quotes:
> We can metaphorically (but like, hopefully it's a good metaphor) think of speech as being [the sum of](/blog/2012/Sep/the-parity-decomposition-trick/) a positive-sum information-conveying component and a zero-sum social-control/memetic-warfare component.
> you _think_ you can punish generalized hate speech without any negative consequences, but you probably won't _notice_ when members of the coalition begin to slowly gerrymander the _hate speech_ category boundary in the service of their own values. Whoops!
