In _The New York Times_ op-ed section [(archive link)](https://archive.is/iNBGr), information science professor [Zeynep Tufekci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeynep_Tufekci) argues that health authorities damaged their own credibility by misleadingly claiming that masks aren't useful for healthy people rather than being forthright about the need to conserve masks for doctors and nurses in the face of a shortage. (Elizabeth van Nostrand and Jim Babcock made [a similar argument on this website the week before last](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/h4vWsBBjASgiQ2pn6/credibility-of-the-cdc-on-sars-cov-2#Discouraged_Use_of_Masks).)
